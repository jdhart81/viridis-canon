# Phase 8 target portfolio

2026-10-11. **Advisory selection, 20 ranked open research questions.** Every initial novelty label is UNASSESSED; these are proposed investigation targets, not established new results. Certification, physical validation, importance and novelty remain separate. No historical proof, certificate, published manuscript, verification gate or streak evidence is changed.

Choose from the portfolio for at least 60% of prospective runs: `ceil(0.6 × attempted runs in the week)`, including partial weeks (5 of 7); use the original selected subject as the organizing area and do not add a new subject or verification route. Outside-portfolio targets carry a one-line value rationale. Search the exact proposed hypotheses and conclusion before formalization. KNOWN targets are skipped; KNOWN_VARIANT targets need a concrete extension to retain priority; LIKELY_NEW targets get preference. Unavailable search or review yields UNASSESSED and never blocks the existing run.

A question is discharged only by a scoped theorem/counterexample or experiment that meets its stated criterion. A strictly stronger, weaker-premise, better-constant, model-identification or informative negative result may count as progress if the exact change is recorded. Renaming a known theorem, copying a definition or proving a scalar tautology does not discharge an item.

Conditional physical-rate targets retain the printed scope: **logical validity given the model, not empirical validation of its assumptions**. PL and PD remain named assumptions unless independently discharged; Phase 6 simulations do not discharge them as universal physical facts.
