# Viridis Research code of conduct

Viridis Research welcomes rigorous disagreement and expects humane conduct.
Community members must:

- critique claims, methods, and evidence without attacking people;
- state uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect consent, privacy, attribution, cultural context, and data sovereignty;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and
  sustained disruption;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest; and
- never pressure another person to weaken a proof, safety, rights, or evidence
  gate.

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
related public or private forums.

Report ordinary concerns to the maintainers. For a concern requiring
confidentiality, use the private contact route identified in `SECURITY.md` and
mark the report **Code of Conduct — confidential**. Do not disclose private or
sensitive information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the community or evidence record. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical, conflicts will be recused, and the
affected person will receive a reason when safety and law permit. Retaliation
against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
