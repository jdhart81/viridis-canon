# External validation

The Viridis Canon is designed to make independent scrutiny easy. Formal
verification, repository traffic, and real-world validation are different
evidence classes and are never combined into one score.

## Evidence ladder

1. **Traffic** — a view, download, clone, or automated index event.
2. **Engagement** — an identifiable question, critique, issue, or review.
3. **Reuse** — an independent derivation, software dependency, citation, or
   reproduced proof.
4. **External validation** — peer acceptance, a documented expert review, an
   independent empirical test, or a successful replication.
5. **Institutional adoption** — a named organization uses the method in a
   continuing research or operational process.

An artifact advances only to the highest rung supported by inspectable
evidence. Anonymous traffic and self-authored links remain traffic.

## Current boundary

The public catalog currently records no external validation for its records.
That is a status field, not a judgment that the work has no value. It means
Viridis has not yet received or verified qualifying outside evidence.

## How to contribute evidence

Open an
[external-validation report](https://github.com/jdhart81/viridis-canon/issues/new?template=external-validation.yml)
for any of the following:

- a clean or failed independent Lean reproduction;
- a formal critique, counterexample, or assumption challenge;
- a citation or downstream reuse;
- a pre-registered or completed empirical test;
- a peer-review decision or named expert assessment;
- a documented institutional pilot.

Include the exact artifact or DOI, environment or method, result, and a durable
link or attachment. Viridis will preserve negative results and failed
reproductions alongside successes.

## What Viridis will report

Weekly reporting keeps raw traffic separate from named engagement, reuse,
validation, and adoption. No download, clone spike, related DOI, or self-post is
described as adoption.
