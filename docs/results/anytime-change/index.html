Anytime-valid change detection for ecological monitoring

Paper: The Anytime Change Certificate: False-alarm control, a late-change penalty, and a bounded erasure budget for continuous monitoring. Justin D. Hart, 2026. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21971041 · PDF · Lean source · certificate

The question

Has this monitoring signal really changed? And if I re-check after every new observation, for as long as the program runs, how do I keep false alarms under control?

The answer, in plain words

Monitoring programs look at data continuously. Re-running a classical test after every new count inflates the false-alarm rate. This result gives a monitor whose probability of ever raising a false alarm is at most α, however long you watch and however often you look.

It also tells you what a late change costs (a log-sized evidence penalty) and bounds how long, on average, a real change takes to detect.

The mathematics

  • Ville's inequality. For a non-negative martingale (Mn) and c > 0: P(∃n: Mn ≥ c) ≤ E[M0]/c.
  • The monitor. Mn = An + tn, with A0 = 0, An = Λn(An−1 + πn), change-time prior πk = 1/(k(k+1)) and tail tn = 1/(n+1). Λn is the likelihood ratio of observation n. Under the null it is a non-negative martingale with M0 = 1.
  • Anytime false-alarm control. P0(∃n: Mn ≥ 1/α) ≤ α.
  • Late-change penalty. The monitor dominates every embedded change-at-ν detector and alarms no later than it; the evidence boundary for a change at ν is log(1/α) + log(ν(ν+1)).
  • Detection delay. If post-change log-likelihood increments are bounded above by zmax with mean D > 0, the first time the evidence sum reaches a satisfies a/D ≤ E[Na] < (a + zmax)/D.

We could not find Ville's inequality in Mathlib (checked master 74e558e on 2026-10-17) and have offered to contribute it upstream.

Assumptions and limits

  • The null is used only through E₀[Λₙ | past] = 1, with Λₙ non-negative, adapted and integrable.
  • The runnable tool implements the single Bernoulli likelihood-ratio process of the paper; other data types need their own likelihood ratios.
  • The paper's title also mentions an erasure budget. That physical reading is part of the paper's model, not of the certificate.
  • Not field-validated: no monitoring program has used it yet.
