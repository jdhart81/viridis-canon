Anytime-valid change detection for ecological monitoring

Monitoring programs look at data continuously. Re-running a classical test after every new count inflates the false-alarm rate. This result gives a monitor whose probability of ever raising a false alarm is at most α, however long you watch and however often you look.

Can a seed supply network meet restoration demand?

Give the tool your seed lots (with quantities), your planting sites (with demand) and which lots may go to which sites, for example under climate-matched provenance rules. It returns the largest share of every site's demand you can meet at the same time, and the sites that limit it.

DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21971057 · certificate Run-130 · function seed-source-bottleneck

A precautionary capacity rule that is provably safe and provably tight

Plan against the floor d = [μ − σ√((1−α)/α)]₊. Whatever the shape of the uncertainty (any non-negative distribution with mean μ and standard deviation σ), the chance that capacity falls below that floor is at most α.
