# Versioned rights-metadata overlay

The sealed scientific artifacts and historical `HOLD_RIGHTS_METADATA` receipt
are preserved unchanged in provenance. On 8 August 2026, Justin D. Hart
delegated responsibility to put the relevant Viridis Research submission
packages in order and make the best release decisions as head scientist and
engineer. Under that direction, this release overlay assigns CC BY 4.0.

Rationale: the paper is intended as open, reproducible research; CC BY 4.0
preserves attribution while permitting verification, reuse, and extension, and
matches the other public-research candidates in this weekly submission set.

This overlay does not grant journal acceptance, empirical validation, canon or
Intelligence Bound spine admission, or authority for an external publication
action. It changes rights and release metadata only. The paper, Lean source,
theorem statements, numerical checker, and scientific claims are unchanged.
