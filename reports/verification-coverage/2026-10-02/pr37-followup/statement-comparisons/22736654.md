\begin{abstract}
We isolate a deterministic reserve identity for monitored conservation action.
If a supplied worst-case deterioration rate is $r$, observations are separated
by at most $m$, and management response takes an additional time $\ell$, the
blind window is $m+\ell$ and the corresponding loss envelope is
$L=r(m+\ell)$. A reserve $B$ remains nonnegative in this surrogate exactly when
$L\leq B$. Response latency consumes $r\ell$ more reserve than instantaneous
response. For a periodic cycle with passive time $p$, active time $a$, and
supplied repair rate $u$, non-increase requires $rp\leq ua$. Exact-rational
tests support the identities; the nine frozen Lean declarations now have exact-hash private dual-kernel
Comparator certification. This is a methods note, not
a calibrated ecological trigger, control-barrier proof, or new thermodynamic
speed limit.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and prior work}
Thermodynamic speed limits bound rates of state transformation using quantities
such as entropy production and dynamical activity \cite{shiraishi2018}. The
present note does not derive or extend those inequalities. It uses ``speed
limit'' only in the operational sense that a bounded rate and a finite reserve
constrain how long a steward can remain blind before acting.

Sampled-data control-barrier research treats continuous dynamics, discrete
control updates, uncertainty, and input delays rigorously
\cite{singletary2020}. Conservation research likewise emphasizes that decision
triggers must match monitoring frequency, system rate of change, response
capacity, and the time needed for action to influence state \cite{debie2018}.
Prediction-informed waiting can improve simulated outcomes in conservation
conflicts under resource constraints, but bad waiting decisions remain risky
\cite{bach2022}. Our object is weaker than all three: a scalar ledger that makes
one hidden-delay assumption visible.

\section{Model and preregistered claims}
Let $r,m,\ell,B,u,p,a\geq0$ be supplied quantities in compatible units. Define
the blind window, blind-window loss, residual reserve, and cycle drift by
\begin{align}
 W &= m+\ell, & L &= rW,\\
 R &= B-L, & \Delta &= rp-ua.
\end{align}
The constant-rate envelope is an assumption, not a fitted ecological law.

\paragraph{Triviality probe.}
Expanding $r(m+\ell)$ and rearranging a linear inequality proves the
load-bearing statements in at most five lines. The disposition is therefore
\texttt{METHODS\_NOTE}.

\paragraph{Proposition 1 (blind-window decomposition).}
The loss envelope separates exactly:
\begin{equation}
 L=r(m+\ell)=rm+r\ell. \label{eq:decomposition}
\end{equation}
Relative to zero response delay, latency consumes exactly $r\ell$ additional
reserve. When $r\geq0$, increasing $\ell$ cannot reduce $L$.

\paragraph{Proposition 2 (reserve condition).}
Residual reserve is nonnegative exactly when
\begin{equation}
 r(m+\ell)\leq B. \label{eq:safety}
\end{equation}
This is a conditional ledger test. It is not a proof that $B$ is an ecological
threshold or that the supplied $r$ bounds the real system.

\paragraph{Proposition 3 (cycle balance).}
If passive deterioration is bounded by $rp$ and active repair is represented by
$ua$, then $rp\leq ua$ implies $\Delta\leq0$. The same condition implies the
undivided active-share form
\begin{equation}
 r(p+a)\leq(r+u)a. \label{eq:share}
\end{equation}
No biological reversibility or constant treatment effect is inferred.

The boundary witness $(r,m,\ell,B,u,p,a)=(2,3,1,8,4,4,2)$ gives
$L=8$, $R=0$, and $\Delta=0$. Holding $B=8$ but setting $\ell=3$ gives
$L=12$ and $R=-4$, the preregistered excessive-latency control.

\section{Discussion, falsification, and limitations}
Equation~\eqref{eq:safety} is useful only after every unit and bound is defined.
Real conservation systems may have observation error, nonlinear or stochastic
deterioration, delayed detection, threshold dynamics, spatial coupling,
seasonality, partial compliance, uncertain intervention effect, or political
and logistical delay. Those effects can enlarge the blind window or invalidate
the linear loss envelope. Prediction can also change the decision problem; it
is absent here.

The mathematical package is falsified by an exact counterexample under its own
definitions or any paper-to-Lean mismatch. Field use would additionally require
validated indicator dynamics, conservative rate estimation, uncertainty
